Kurukshetra — Privacy Policy

App: Kurukshetra (com.asish.kurukshetra) · Last updated: 23 November 2026
Short version: Kurukshetra does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The game works fully offline. Your progress and settings stay only on your own phone.

1. What data the app collects

None. Kurukshetra does not ask for an account or login, and does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

2. What is saved on your phone

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves a few small values in the app's own local storage on your device:

  • Stars earned in each story day and which days are unlocked
  • Your chosen warrior and which help cards you have already seen
  • Settings: sound volumes and graphics quality

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it completely.

3. Internet, ads and third parties

The game, its voices, pictures and fonts are all inside the app and it needs no internet connection to play. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

4. Permissions

The app does not request any special permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access). It keeps the screen awake while you play.

5. Children

Kurukshetra is an action game based on the Mahabharata and is intended for players aged 13 and above. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features such as online play or sign-in, this policy will be updated on this page before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
